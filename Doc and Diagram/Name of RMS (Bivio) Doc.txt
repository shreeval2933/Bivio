Top recommendation: Bivio

Bivio is Italian (from Latin bivium) for a fork in the road — the point where one path splits into two.

The story writes itself: "A candidate arrives at one profile and one AI score, then the road forks — permanent employment down one branch, project-based contracting down the other. The interesting engineering was deciding exactly where the shared path ends, and it ends at the point of commitment: applications and proposals diverge, and everything downstream does too."

That answer demonstrates you understood your own architecture, which is what the question is really probing. It's short, pronounceable (BEE-vee-oh), spells cleanly, and I found no meaningful collision in hiring or HR tech.
